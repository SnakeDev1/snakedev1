# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS deployed through GitHub Pages.

## Users

Minecraft community owners, administrators, staff members, and players who use the SnakeMC Discord application.

## Product Purpose

SnakeMC connects Discord communities with Minecraft server operations. This site provides the public legal and trust pages required to evaluate and use the application.

## Operating Context

Visitors reach these pages from the Discord Developer Portal, the SnakeMC application profile, or the support flow. They need concise, readable information about service terms, data processing, retention, and deletion requests.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site must work as static files on GitHub Pages.
- Terms and privacy pages must have stable public HTTPS URLs.
- Legal copy must reflect the bot's actual behavior and be updated when its data practices change.
- Contact is currently provided through the Discord account `snakedev.` until a dedicated support email or server URL is supplied.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: SnakeMC.
- Parent identity: SnakeCode.
- Existing icon asset: `assets/snakemc-logo-icon-v2.png`.
- Brand colors: deep navy, electric blue, and turquoise.
- Voice: direct, trustworthy, and understandable Turkish.

## Evidence on Hand

- Original SnakeMC logo assets are present in `assets/`.
- No testimonials, customer counts, uptime claims, or certifications have been supplied and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Explain data use plainly.
- Collect and retain only what the service needs.
- Make support and deletion requests easy to understand.
- Keep the legal pages fast, accessible, and readable on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus states, sufficient contrast, responsive typography, and reduced-motion support.
